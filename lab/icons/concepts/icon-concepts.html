icon concepts — four complete looks, each with its own way of drawing

constellation

the item as a star figure: dots joined by thin lines, the way Cancer, Lepus and Pisces are drawn. cooked = three sparks rise off the figure · fine = the brightest star twinkles

token

a board piece: the cube glyph seen from above, the item stamped flat on its top face. cooked = the face fires to ink and the mark shows in paper · fine = a second rim inside the face

brush

two or three tapered strokes, a dab and a flick — one gesture per item. cooked = smaller, lower, two steam flicks · fine = a small crossed gleam

contour

terraces: the silhouette as nested rings, like the elevation terraces on the board. cooked = solid with the rings cut in paper · fine = finer terraces
